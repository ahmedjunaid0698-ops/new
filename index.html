ABOUT AUTOLINK

Making Vehicle Communication Smarter.

AutoLink is a smart vehicle communication concept designed to solve everyday problems such as blocked vehicles, parking issues and urgent communication.

Instead of exposing a vehicle owner's personal phone number, AutoLink creates a controlled communication layer between the two people.

OUR VISION

Every vehicle should have a simple, secure and smart way to communicate.
